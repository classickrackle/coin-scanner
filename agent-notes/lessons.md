# Lessons (max 15 bullets)
- 2026-10-12 (updated 2026-11-18): Skip means not researchable or not tradeable, not a direction call: skips swing both ways (+17% at 7 days on 10-12, but -44% at 30 days on 10-19, Zip coin -98%). Never treat skip as a short signal or caution as a hold.
- 2026-10-12 (merged 2026-11-20): Require a dated, verified catalyst; a sector story, a known name, thin 'news' or a low price vs ATH is never enough (Kaia worth a look 10-11, later -46%).
- 2026-10-15 (updated 2026-11-24): Scanner "new listing" badges are usually old (Walrus, OriginTrail, FLOCK, Grass, Berachain, Axelar, Nillion, Falcon, WalletConnect, Prometeus, BAT, Obol/HTX, Beldex/Pionex from Feb 2026; SKALE/Gemini dates from 2021), and real listings can reverse (Centrifuge, Dolomite). Treat them as old until an announcement within 30 days is found, and re-check them every few days.
- 2026-10-17 (updated 2026-11-23): A verified catalyst that has already moved the price (Puffer +70%, Sanctum/Upbit +60% in a week, then -14% by 11-23) is not a buy signal; for fresh launchpad sales, the sale price is the reference (Mori 3.8x its sale price in 10 days).
- 2026-10-22 (updated 2026-11-24): A volume spike alone is not a catalyst, and unverifiable 'back' triggers count as unconfirmed (HASH, Arkham, META, PolySwarm, Zignaly, Pons-Bithumb and most November 'back' spikes). Clear case: NEM spiked 159% on 11-17 with no news and was -52% seven days later.
- 2026-10-20: Inkbox email needs re-authorization (still refused 2026-11-24); delivery falls back to push/chat.
- 2026-10-27: Check what the token actually is first: regulated fiat stablecoins (AUDM, QCAD), tokenized silver, and tokenized funds/stocks (Ondo, Robinhood USO) are not coins to rate; skip them.
- 2026-11-05: Spiking tokens that rebrand, migrate or contract-swap (Niza 1500:1) have unreliable ATH numbers; check the migration before using athX.
- 2026-11-06 (updated 2026-11-16): Network shutdowns (Blast), DAO wind-downs (Goldfinch, voted 2026-06-23, spiked 40x volume on 11-21), exploited projects (Harmony, Avici, Rhea), chain shutdown votes (Acala, filed 2026-11-11) and exchange delistings (Enzyme, Hooked, Hashflow on Binance) outweigh any volume spike; skip. Hooked (skip 10-17) went on to lose 83% in 30 days.
- 2026-11-10: Worth-a-look record is poor: about 80% of the 10-01 to 10-11 worth-a-look calls fell by 30 days (scorecard: -19.1% vs market -8.9%), with single outliers (Umbra, Bubblemaps, NextPlace) lifting averages. Keep the bar strict and caution as default.
- 2026-11-10: Web search returns almost nothing dated for tiny coins; no verifiable dated catalyst means caution or skip, and 'No top picks' is the correct default, not a failure (44 days running on 2026-11-24).
- 2026-11-12 (rewritten 2026-11-24): Caution calls trailed the market in 12 of the 13 windows logged from 10-17 to 11-16, usually by 3 to 12 points (the 11-14 exception came from one outlier, Radix +74%; averages elsewhere were inflated by 0xMarkets +624% and Elsa +69%). Medians are the honest measure: 10-25 caution at 30 days median -21% vs market -24%, 11-17 at 7 days median 0% vs -6%. Caution is not a hold signal; ratings only add value through rare, verified catalysts.
- 2026-11-16: Established networks with no catalyst are not even caution: Mina (-44%), Flow (-52%) and Hyperlane (-47%), all caution on 10-12, hit their 'wrong if' within 5 weeks. A known name with no dated event should be skip.
- 2026-11-20: Token-unlock dates are red flags to check on the day: Sapien (unlock 11-20) and CYBER (unlock mid-November) both came on the list with most supply still locked; a scheduled unlock within 2 weeks rules out worth a look.
- 2026-11-17: 'No longer in CoinGecko data' can mean a token migration, not a delisting (Clearpool CPOOL to CLEAR, swap opened 11-17); search for a migration before calling a tracked coin dead.
